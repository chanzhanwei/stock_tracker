        Data and indicators

        Data sources

        • Daily prices come from the Yahoo Finance chart API. If that call is empty or fails, the chart uses a deterministic mock random walk. The badge says yahoo or mock.
        • Earnings and dividends use Yahoo when a calendar or dividend field is available. Any missing label is mock-filled. AGM is mock-filled when Yahoo does not provide one.
        • The institutional buy/sell split is always mock data. It is labeled mock in the panel.

        How a vote becomes a verdict

        Each active implemented indicator votes −1, 0, or +1. Placeholder rows vote N/A and are left out. The overall score is the mean of the votes that count. BUY if the mean is above 0.2, SELL if it is below −0.2, otherwise HOLD. Confidence is round(50 + mean × 50)%.

        Glossary

        • SMA 20/50. Buy when close > SMA 20 > SMA 50. Sell when close < SMA 20 < SMA 50. If only one of the two is on, that average is compared with the close.
        • Custom SMA. Buy when close is above SMA(n). Sell when it is below.
        • EMA 21. Buy when close is above the 21-day exponential average. Sell when it is below.
        • VWAP. Cumulative typical-price average weighted by volume. Buy when close is above VWAP. Sell when it is below.
        • RSI 14. Buy below 30. Sell above 70.
        • MACD 12/26/9. Buy when the MACD line is above the signal line. Sell when it is below.
        • Bollinger (20, 2). Buy at or below the lower band. Sell at or above the upper band.
        • Volume bars. Buy when volume is above 1.3× the previous 20-bar average on an up day. Sell on a down day with that volume.
        • Institutional. Buy when buy% exceeds sell% by more than 15 points. Sell when sell% exceeds buy% by more than 15. Mock only.
        • Placeholders. Ichimoku, PSAR, ADX/DMI, Stoch, CCI, Will%R, ROC, Keltner, Donchian, ATR, OBV, MFI, A/D Line, Pivots, and Fibonacci stay listed and vote N/A.
