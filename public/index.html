      Data and indicators

      Data sources

      • Daily prices come from the Yahoo Finance chart API. Mock data is optional and off until the Mock data switch is on. With the switch off and no Yahoo bars, the desk says “No live prices. Mock data is off.” With the switch on, a gap is filled by a deterministic mock random walk. The badge says yahoo, mock, or none.
      • Earnings and dividends use Yahoo when a calendar or dividend field is available. Mock fills, including the mock AGM, are added only while Mock data is on.
      • The institutional buy/sell split is mock data, and only while Mock data is on. With the switch off the panel says institutional data is off, and that vote stays out of the average.

      How a vote becomes a verdict

      Each active implemented indicator votes −1, 0, or +1. Off and N/A stay out. The verdict is the largest count of buy, hold, or sell. A tie is HOLD. Confidence is that count divided by the active signals. The slider sits on the left for a sell, the middle for a hold, and the right for a buy.

      Glossary

      • SMA 20/50. Buy when close > SMA 20 > SMA 50. Sell when close < SMA 20 < SMA 50. If only one of the two is on, that average is compared with the close.
      • Custom SMA. Buy when close is above SMA(n). Sell when it is below.
      • EMA 21. Buy when close is above the 21-day exponential average. Sell when it is below.
      • VWAP. Cumulative typical-price average weighted by volume. Buy when close is above VWAP. Sell when it is below.
      • RSI 14. Buy below 30. Sell above 70.
      • MACD 12/26/9. Buy when the MACD line is above the signal line. Sell when it is below.
      • Bollinger (20, 2). Buy at or below the lower band. Sell at or above the upper band.
      • Volume bars. Buy when volume is above 1.3× the previous 20-bar average on an up day. Sell on a down day with that volume.
      • Institutional. Buy when buy% exceeds sell% by more than 15 points. Sell when sell% exceeds buy% by more than 15. Shown only while Mock data is on.
      • Placeholders. Ichimoku, PSAR, ADX/DMI, Stoch, CCI, Will%R, ROC, Keltner, Donchian, ATR, OBV, MFI, A/D Line, Pivots, and Fibonacci stay listed and vote N/A.
